01 · What we found on Facebook

Allowed, but not the best use of money in November

  • You're allowed to advertise there. An in-person practice doesn't need special approval. The rules: no drug names, no ads that suggest the viewer has a condition, and adults only.
  • No local doctor is doing it. We searched Meta's ad library. No primary care practice in Huntington Beach is running Facebook ads right now. Nobody has proven it works here yet.
  • Medicare ads on Facebook have a trust problem. Scam Medicare ads have flooded Facebook, and seniors have learned to tune them out. Medicare enrollment also closes Dec 7, which leaves us about three weeks.
  • It costs more per call. Facebook typically runs several times what Google costs per call. Your Google calls are running about $10 each.
